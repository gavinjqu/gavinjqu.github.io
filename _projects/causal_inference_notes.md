Applied causal inference techniques from an economics masters course, refactored from R into Python. Five labs replicate canonical empirical studies — including Chattopadhyay & Duflo (2004), Card & Krueger (1994), and Acemoglu, Johnson & Robinson (2001) — each available as both a Jupyter notebook and a Quarto document.
